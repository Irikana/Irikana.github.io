A Place for All Knowledge

June Second

Author: KeDao Xue
Created: June 2, 2026
Type: Handwritten Article
Tags: None

I love my friends, I love to study, I hope things are going well.

I know what the most important thing in my life is.

I am willing to work hard for what matters most in my life.

I think I am a complete person. I have dreams, and I am working on them—even if it's just a little bit each day, I am living for that.

But sometimes, recently, I feel like the world is not prepared for me.

I want to have long hair and style it like a girl's. I want to wear girls' clothes, and I truly want to understand girls: curious about what it feels like to be them, and how they think.

I used to think this was a kind of love, but then I changed my mind. The love people talk about is boring, and completely different from what I feel. If I had to name it, I'd say it's more like a child's love, but more extreme.

It feels a little strange. I think most people around me are strange. Boys like girls, they might fall in love, but they never truly become like each other. Maybe this is just my prejudice; I don't talk much with people.

I haven't tried to fall in love. I used to have feelings and emotions, but actually no one talks to me that much.

I am sad, I feel pathetic, and anxious. But I am still going. Even if I just write down a few words every day, it is a step forward. And I have been sticking to this, every single day.
