Woke Up Late

Author: KeDao Xue
Created: April 10, 2026
Type: Handwritten Article
Tags: None

My first time to wake up late, too late to get to class.

It is very strange and unexpected, because I didn't go to sleep very late relatively, and I had almost the same length of sleep, relatively.

The class is mathematical methods in physics. After I arrived, the class had already begun one minute, and I don't even know if it is my lucky or unlucky at this moment.

I can't control my brain, to think, imagine.

When I am (currently yeah I am) sitting in the classroom, I would immediately start thinking and imagining, which I think it is addicted for me but it is actually harmful to study in class.

My body would become hard when I start it in a class, which is very terrible. I thus can't really hear the class, but only myself, or things happening in my mind. These weird things have existed for years, and I don't really know how to fix it. The only thing I did or have been doing is, keep writing down something or draw something to let my body move. But it is still useless, completely useless for school.
